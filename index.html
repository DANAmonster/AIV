About us

Air Indea Virtual is built for pilots who like flying India’s busiest routes the way real crews do: proper procedures, live ATC and honest PIREPs.

Whether you’re new to Infinite Flight or already a regular, there’s a seat for you. We keep things friendly, active and fair.

Our hubs

Start your flights from any of our three hubs.

  • VIDPDelhi, Indira Gandhi International
  • VOBLBengaluru, Kempegowda International
  • VABBMumbai, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International

How to join

  1. Apply with your Infinite Flight accountSign in on our application page. We check the account you actually fly with.
  2. Answer the knowledge questionsA short quiz on airports, squawk codes, speeds and approaches.
  3. Wait for staff reviewOur team reviews every application and replies with a decision.
  4. Get your Discord inviteAccepted pilots get a welcome message with a private invite to meet the crew.
